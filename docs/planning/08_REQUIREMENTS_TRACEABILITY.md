## 1. Overview & Verification Strategy

The Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) establishes bidirectional traceability between every scientific requirement specified in the research paper by Dubey et al. (2026), the product brief requirements, the planned software modules, candidate datasets, implementation phases, and automated acceptance tests.

All performance expectations are categorized according to standard engineering states: **[Proposed Target]**, **[Untested Engineering Estimate]**, or **[Measured Fact]**.
